# Google Sheets

You can export your scraper results to Google sheets. It's quick and easy.

First, navigate to your scraper from the dashboard at https://fetchfox.ai/dashboard.

Then, click the results button to bring up your export options.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2025-03-24 at 23.30.44@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>The "Results" button will open the export menu</p></figcaption></figure>

The first time you run an export to Google Sheets, you will need to connect your account. Click the button and approve the FetchFox connection.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2025-03-24 at 23.33.38@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>You'll need to connect Google the first time</p></figcaption></figure>

After connecting Google, there's two ways to run the export. The first is a one-off. For this, just click the button that says "Export to Google Sheets"

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2025-03-24 at 23.34.57@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>You can trigger a one-off export in the app</p></figcaption></figure>

If you prefer, you can ask FetchFox to automatically export to Google Sheets each time you run the scraper. For this, just flip the switch.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2025-03-24 at 23.34.49@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>You can ask FetchFox to automatically export to Google Sheets.</p></figcaption></figure>
